# Design Patterns

> Reusable solutions such as Singleton, Factory, Observer and the SOLID principles.
> Category: CS Fundamentals. Typical effort: about 6 study days.

## SOLID principles

- **Single responsibility** — a class has one reason to change.
- **Open-closed** — open for extension and closed for modification.
- **Dependency inversion** — depend on abstractions not concrete classes.

Practice: Refactor a class that does too many jobs.

## Creational patterns

- **Singleton** — guarantees one instance of a class.
- **Factory** — creates objects without exposing construction logic.
- **Builder** — constructs complex objects step by step.

Practice: Replace scattered new calls with a factory.

## Behavioural patterns

- **Observer** — notifies subscribers when state changes.
- **Strategy** — swaps algorithms behind a common interface.
- **Decorator** — adds behaviour by wrapping an object.

Practice: Build a notification system with Observer.

## Mini project

Implement Singleton, Factory, Observer and Strategy in a small app and document when each is appropriate.
