# R

> Statistical programming language for data analysis and visualization.
> Category: Programming. Typical effort: about 10 study days.

## R basics

- **Vector** — ordered collection of same typed values.
- **Data frame** — table of rows and columns.
- **Tidyverse** — collection of packages for data analysis.

Practice: Load a CSV and summarize it.

## Visualization and models

- **ggplot2** — grammar of graphics plotting package.
- **dplyr** — verbs to filter, group and summarize data.
- **lm** — fits a linear regression model.

Practice: Plot a relationship and fit a regression.
