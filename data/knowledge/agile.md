# Agile

> Iterative delivery with sprints, stand-ups, backlogs and retrospectives.
> Category: Tools. Typical effort: about 2 study days.

## Agile and Scrum basics

- **Sprint** — fixed length iteration that delivers a working increment.
- **Product backlog** — ordered list of everything the product needs.
- **User story** — requirement written from the user's point of view.

Practice: Write five user stories with acceptance criteria.

## Scrum events and roles

- **Daily stand-up** — short daily sync on progress and blockers.
- **Retrospective** — team reflection on how to improve.
- **Product owner** — person who prioritizes the backlog.

Practice: Plan a sprint and hold a mock retrospective.
