# Data Warehousing

> Star schemas, fact and dimension tables and warehouse platforms such as Snowflake and BigQuery.
> Category: Data & ML. Typical effort: about 6 study days.

## Warehouse concepts

- **Data warehouse** — central store optimized for analytics.
- **OLTP** — transaction processing for day to day operations.
- **OLAP** — analytical processing across large datasets.

Practice: Compare OLTP and OLAP for a retail business.

## Dimensional modeling

- **Fact table** — stores measurable events such as sales.
- **Dimension table** — descriptive context such as product or date.
- **Star schema** — fact table linked directly to dimensions.

Practice: Design a star schema for orders.

## Mini project

Design a star schema for sales analytics, load sample data and write five analytical queries.
