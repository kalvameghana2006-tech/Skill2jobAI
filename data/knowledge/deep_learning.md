# Deep Learning

> Neural networks with backpropagation, CNNs and sequence models.
> Category: Data & ML. Typical effort: about 20 study days.

## Neural network foundations

- **Neuron** — computes a weighted sum then applies an activation.
- **Backpropagation** — propagates errors backwards to update weights.
- **Gradient descent** — iteratively adjusts weights to reduce loss.

Practice: Train a tiny network on a toy dataset.

## Training deep models

- **Epoch** — one full pass over the training data.
- **Dropout** — randomly disables neurons to reduce overfitting.
- **Learning rate** — step size for weight updates.

Practice: Compare two learning rates and plot loss.

## CNNs and sequences

- **Convolution** — filter sliding over an image to detect patterns.
- **Pooling** — downsampling that keeps the strongest features.
- **LSTM** — recurrent unit designed for long sequences.

Practice: Build a CNN that classifies digits.

## Mini project

Train a small CNN on MNIST or CIFAR-10 and report accuracy curves, an ablation and error analysis.
