# OOP

> Modelling software with classes, encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism and abstraction.
> Category: CS Fundamentals. Typical effort: about 5 study days.

## Classes and objects

- **Class** — blueprint defining fields and methods.
- **Object** — an instance of a class.
- **Constructor** — special method that initialises a new object.

Practice: Model a Student and a Course class.

## Four pillars

- **Encapsulation** — bundle data with methods and hide internals.
- **Abstraction** — expose what matters and hide complexity.
- **Polymorphism** — same call, different behaviour depending on the object.

Practice: Create a Shape hierarchy that demonstrates polymorphism.

## Design basics

- **Composition** — building objects from other objects.
- **Interface** — contract of methods without implementation.
- **SOLID** — five principles for maintainable object-oriented design.

Practice: Refactor a class with too many responsibilities.

## Mini project

Model a small system (parking lot, library) with at least five classes showing all four OOP pillars, and explain your design in a README.
