# NLP

> Working with text: tokenization, embeddings, classification and transformer models.
> Category: Data & ML. Typical effort: about 12 study days.

## Text preprocessing

- **Tokenization** — splitting text into tokens.
- **Stop words** — very common words often removed.
- **Stemming** — reducing words to a crude root form.

Practice: Clean and tokenize a review dataset.

## Representations

- **TF-IDF** — weights words by frequency and rarity.
- **Word embedding** — dense vector capturing meaning.
- **Cosine similarity** — measures the angle between two vectors.

Practice: Find the most similar documents to a query.

## Transformers

- **Attention** — lets a model weigh which words matter for each other.
- **BERT** — pretrained transformer encoder for language understanding.
- **Fine-tuning** — adapting a pretrained model to a task.

Practice: Fine-tune a small model on a labelled dataset.

## Mini project

Build a text classifier (sentiment or spam) with TF-IDF baseline and a transformer fine-tune, comparing metrics.
