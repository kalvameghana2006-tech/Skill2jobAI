# Statistics

> Distributions, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals and regression for data-driven decisions.
> Category: Data & ML. Typical effort: about 10 study days.

## Descriptive statistics

- **Mean** — arithmetic average.
- **Median** — middle value, robust to outliers.
- **Standard deviation** — typical distance of values from the mean.

Practice: Summarise a dataset with mean, median and spread.

## Probability and distributions

- **Probability** — number between 0 and 1 measuring how likely an event is.
- **Normal distribution** — symmetric bell-shaped distribution.
- **Independence** — one event does not change the probability of another.

Practice: Simulate coin flips and dice to see the law of large numbers.

## Inference

- **Hypothesis test** — procedure deciding if data supports a claim.
- **p-value** — probability of data at least this extreme if the null were true.
- **Confidence interval** — range likely to contain the true value.

Practice: Run a t-test and interpret the p-value in plain words.

## Regression

- **Correlation** — strength of a linear relationship.
- **Regression coefficient** — change in outcome for a one unit change in a predictor.
- **R-squared** — share of variance explained by the model.

Practice: Fit a regression and check residuals.

## Mini project

Analyse a dataset end to end: descriptive stats, a hypothesis test with interpretation, and a regression, written up for a non-technical reader.
